I've never done Pilates. Can I still come?

Yes. Most of our clients start as beginners. Your coach shows you everything on the reformer, step by step.

Is it hard?

It's as hard as you want it to be. Every move can be made easier or stronger, and nobody is watching but your coach.

What should I wear?

Comfortable, fitted clothes and grip socks. Forgot your socks? The boutique has you covered.

How big are the classes?

Small, on purpose. Few enough that your coach can correct every rep.
